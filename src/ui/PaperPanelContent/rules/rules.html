TOUCH CONTROLS

Play with your phone sideways. Touch the lower-left area to reveal the movement arrows. Drag up or down to walk, sideways to turn, and farther to run. Release to hide the arrows.

Drag on the right side to look around. Tap Jump to jump. When an object is nearby, tap its action button. In focused views, use the arrows, Details and Back. The cauldron starts brewing as soon as you open it.
